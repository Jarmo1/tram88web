Numinbah Road

A tram that stopped
and stayed.

You'll spot it from the road: a deep maroon tram sitting on a green pad between the cane and the water, awning out, stools under an umbrella. Pull in. Order at the window. Sit down and look at the mountains for a while.

There's no rush here, which is the point. Breakfast runs into lunch, the coffee comes out properly made, and the chip sandwich is only on the menu if you ask for it.

Atmosphere like you're visiting your aunty who is a farmer and hasn't seen you for a while. Anthony Butov · Google review

Shamrock & Smoke

Same tram, different week. Through the working week the window opens before sunrise as Shamrock & Smoke. Irish roots, hardwood in the firebox, and a coffee ready before you've finished parking.

It's built for early starts: tradies, farmers, riders and anyone heading up the valley. Same stools, same view, just quieter and a good deal earlier.
